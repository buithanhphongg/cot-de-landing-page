# Cốt Đê design direction

The site should feel like a small independent tech publication made by two real people. Keep the founders' photo as the primary visual evidence of who they are.

## Visual rules

- Warm paper (`#f3f0e8`), dark forest ink (`#19241d`), and one terracotta accent (`#c15b35`).
- Use Be Vietnam Pro for Vietnamese readability and DM Mono only for small labels and figure notes.
- Favor left-aligned editorial columns, open space, fine rules, and numbered sections.
- Avoid decorative gradients, glows, glass effects, stock illustrations, fake metrics, and repeated cards.
- Let type size, image scale, and content order create hierarchy. Motion should be subtle and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Content rules

- Describe what Cốt Đê actually covers. Do not invent audience counts, publishing schedules, testimonials, or credentials.
- Keep Vietnamese and English copy aligned in meaning, without forcing literal translations.
- Maintain copy in `src/site-data.js`; avoid inserting text or channel URLs directly into layout code.
- New sections must work at narrow widths, with keyboard focus and useful image alt text.
